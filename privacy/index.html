Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Penguin Music Studio handles information when authorized studio operators use Penguin Short Publisher, an internal web tool for connecting TikTok and publishing original short-form music content through TikTok's official developer APIs.

1. Information we process

When an authorized TikTok account is connected, Penguin Short Publisher may process the information needed to authenticate the connection and perform requested publishing actions, including:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information made available through approved API scopes.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens issued by TikTok.
  • Video publishing metadata such as captions, privacy selections, post identifiers, status, and API response information.
  • Operational logs needed to diagnose failed requests or maintain the publishing workflow.

Penguin Short Publisher does not collect or store TikTok account passwords.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to operate the internal publishing workflow, including authenticating authorized TikTok accounts, submitting approved content, checking publishing status, maintaining the connection, and troubleshooting technical issues.

3. Token and credential security

OAuth credentials and tokens are handled server-side. Sensitive values are not intentionally exposed in public pages or client-side application code. We use reasonable technical safeguards appropriate for an internal publishing system, including restricted access and encrypted storage where implemented.

4. Data sharing

Information is shared with TikTok when necessary to authenticate the account or perform requested API operations. Penguin Music Studio does not sell personal information obtained through Penguin Short Publisher.

5. Retention

We retain authorization data, publishing metadata, and operational records only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, or meet applicable legal obligations. Authorized connections may be revoked or removed when they are no longer required.

6. Third-party services

Penguin Short Publisher relies on TikTok's platform, OAuth services, and developer APIs. TikTok's own terms and privacy practices apply to information processed by TikTok.

7. Access and deletion requests

Authorized users may request removal of stored account connection data associated with Penguin Short Publisher by contacting Penguin Music Studio at penguinmusicstudio@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, APIs, security practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
